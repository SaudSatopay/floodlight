The twist

The same ward floods for two different reasons.

Genuine rainfall overload — or a blocked drain, even in moderate rain. A system that only watches rainfall completely misses the second, more common cause. FLOODLIGHT continuously compares what the rain should be doing against what people and sensors actually see.

The pipeline

Listen. Expect. Compare. Warn.

01

LISTEN

15-minute AWS rain windows, the tide clock, geotagged WhatsApp photo reports — each stamped with a CV depth-band estimate — and an optional ₹2k ultrasonic level node.

02

EXPECT

A per-street excess-rain model: drain capacity × bowl factor × the tide-lock multiplier that seals Mumbai's outfalls past ~3 m.

03

COMPARE

Expected vs observed, street by street. Small gap → Cause A. Large gap → Cause B: the blockage belief updates Bayesian-style and flows back into EXPECT.

04

WARN

Street alerts in Marathi, Hindi and English with real lead time. Dispatch cards carry the drain ID and the evidence — not a hunch.
